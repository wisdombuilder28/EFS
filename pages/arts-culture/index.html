Arts & Culture

At EFS, we believe every child has a hidden talent waiting to be discovered. Our Arts & Culture programme creates the space for students to explore creativity, express themselves, and develop confidence through Brigade, drama, and visual arts.

Our Creative Clubs

  • Brigade Club — discipline, teamwork, and leadership
  • Drama Club — public speaking and performance
  • Art Classes — drawing, painting, and crafts
  • Cultural performances and festivals
  • Community and social awareness projects

Why Arts Matter

  • Builds creativity and lateral thinking
  • Develops public speaking and confidence
  • Promotes cultural identity and pride
  • Encourages teamwork and collaboration
  • Regular inter-school competitions
